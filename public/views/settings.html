Check for lost Fakemon Compares your collection against this device’s backup copies and restores anything missing. If nothing is missing here and you are signed in, it opens your cloud backup so you can check that too.
Export your collection Download every Fakemon you own as a backup file you can re-import later. Available from My Collection → Export Collection.
Cloud Backup
Back up to your account Keep a private copy of your collection on our server so you can get it back on a new device or after clearing your browser. Only you can see it, and it is separate from publishing to the community hub. You choose exactly which Fakémon, moves, abilities and items go in, up to the allowance shown above.
Restore from your backup Copies the entries you tick onto this device. Anything already here is left alone unless you tick it, in which case the backed-up version replaces it. Nothing is ever deleted.
Delete your backup Removes the copy stored on our server. Your collection on this device is untouched.
