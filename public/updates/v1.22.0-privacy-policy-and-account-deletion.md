title: Privacy Policy and Account Deletion
---

- Added a full Privacy Policy, at dex.woogi.xyz/privacy. It says plainly what we collect, what we don't (no ads, no analytics, no tracking you around the web), and who the site talks to.
- The Terms of Service now have a page of their own too, at dex.woogi.xyz/terms, instead of only a pop-up. Both are linked from the bottom of the sidebar, next to the Community Rules.
- You can now delete your account, from Settings > Account. Nothing is destroyed for seven days, so a misclick is not the end of the world, and you can call it off from the same place at any point that week.
- Export your collection first if you go that route. Collection > Export downloads the lot as a file you can import again later.
