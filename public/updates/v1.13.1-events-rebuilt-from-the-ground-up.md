title: Events, Rebuilt From the Ground Up
---

- **Events can be anything now.** Poster competitions, mascot contests, PoA contests, tournament sign-ups and more, all in the Community Hub's Events tab.
- **Entering is like filling in a form.** Some events ask for text, images, links or one of your Fakémon. Your email and Discord fill themselves in if they're on your account, and Fakémon you enter don't count toward your uploads or cloud storage.
- **Bring a Fakémon from anywhere.** Import a Woogidex file or a text export, fill in anything it's missing, and preview it before you send it in. You can edit your entry until entries close.
- **Enter moves, abilities, items and types too.** Pick one you've made, import it, or make one right on the form.
- **Event rules are checked for you.** If an event needs artwork or bans custom types, you'll see it before you submit.
- **Your answers are saved as you go**, and you'll be asked before leaving a form you haven't finished.
- **Times show in your own time zone.**
- **New ways to vote.** Depending on the event, you can rate every entry, hand out a few votes, or leave it to the judges.
- If an organizer removes your entry, you'll get a notification telling you why.
- **Got an entry code?** Some organizers hand out codes for extra entries. Enter yours on the event page.
- **Share events anywhere.** Repost an event to your feed or profile, or share its link. Some events are even open to people without an account. Event links now show the event's name and cover when you paste them.
- **A fresh look for events.** Events now show as tickets. Open one to read about it, and entering or voting opens on a page of its own.
- **Spoilers and more.** Posts and comments now support ||spoilers||, code blocks and dividing lines.
- **Battles are taking a break** while I build a better simulator.
- **Woogidex opens even when the servers are down**, so your collection is always there.
- You can now crop region banners to fit.
- Collection card buttons are now stacked vertically, and the no-artwork placeholder looks right in dark mode.
