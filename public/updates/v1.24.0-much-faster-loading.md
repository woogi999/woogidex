version: v1.24.0
date: August 31, 2026
title: Much Faster Loading
---

- Fixed a bug that made the site re-download about 3.7 MB of Pokemon data on every single visit, even though it already had a copy saved. Second and later visits are dramatically faster now.
- Community Hub artwork is remembered between visits instead of being downloaded again every time you open the hub.
- Opening the same Fakemon twice no longer downloads it twice, and the like and comment counters no longer load every like and comment just to count them.
- Notifications stop polling while the tab is in the background, and only fetch the full list when you actually open the panel.
