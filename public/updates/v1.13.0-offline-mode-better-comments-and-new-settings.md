---
version: v1.13.0
date: October 5, 2026
title: Offline Mode, Better Comments and a Brand New Settings Page
---

- **Woogidex works offline now!** Visit once with a connection and your collection, the editor, analysis, regions and exports all keep working with no internet at all. Community stuff, messages and battles still need a connection, and the site tells you when you've dropped off.
- **Way faster on older phones and laptops.** The site downloads a lot less when it opens, and the heavy stuff (battles, PNG exports) only loads when you actually use it. Slower devices also get lighter effects automatically, so scrolling stays smooth.
- **Comment changes** Tap the comment button on anything in the feed and the comments open right there under it, no new page. You can **edit your own comments** now, and **react to any comment** with Like or any of our emojis.
- **Profiles: your wall and your posts are one timeline.** Everything you post, everything you publish, and everything people write on your wall, all together, newest first. Visiting someone? "Write something to them…" sits right at the top.
- **Search finds people!** Type a name or @username in the search bar to jump straight to someone's profile.
- **A brand new Settings page.** Sections down the side, a search box to find any setting in a second, a light/dark theme picker, and a new **This device** section that shows how much is saved here, whether your browser is protecting it, and whether offline mode is ready.
- **Full-screen editor.** Hit the expand button at the top of the editor (or flip it on in Settings) and it takes up the whole window instead of the right side.
- **The Community Hub has a sidebar** for Feed, Browse, Events and My uploads.
- **Fixed "Your Collection Did Not Load" for good.** For some people it came back on every reload and blocked saving anything. That's fixed, and if your browser ever acts up anyway, you can now switch the check off from the warning itself or from Settings > Data.
- **Fixed** not being able to upload or change your profile's cover photo, and the Regions menu on phones ignoring every tap after you opened it.
- View counts on published Fakémon are hidden now. They still help the feed decide what to show you, they're just not on display.
