- **BOO. Woogidex looks completely different now.** Cleaner, calmer, way less clutter. You're welcome.
- **Everything's one click away.** Collection, Community Hub, Battle and Updates live along the top now. On phones they hop down to a bar at the bottom, like a real app!
- **A search bar!!** Find pages, your Fakemon, moves, abilities, items and types in a snap. Signed in? It searches Community Hub posts too. Hit Enter to see every single result.
- **REGIONS. Build your own Kanto.** A region holds its own Fakemon, moves, abilities, items and types, and you pick what it borrows from the main games (by default: no Pokémon, and the National Dex's moves, items, abilities and types).
- **Remix the main games inside your region.** Click any main-game Pokémon, move, item, ability or type in a region to make your region's own version: a beefier Flamethrower, a Fire type with new weaknesses, a Charmander with a whole new moveset. It only changes things in that region.
- **Evolution lines fill themselves in.** Make a Fakemon from a real Pokémon and its whole family shows up on the evolution board, methods and all.
- **Region details:** a name, a tagline, a banner, a bio. Make it yours.
- **Region analytics!!** Type spread, stat totals, roles, speed tiers, shared weaknesses, move and ability breakdowns... basically everything a nerd could want (said with love).
- **Export a region all by itself**, as a file you can import again, a Showdown mod, an Essentials mod or plain text.
- **CUSTOM TYPES.** Invent your own types with their own colour (gradients too!), icon and matchups, then slap them on your Fakemon and moves. Plus a full type chart.
- **Pictures for custom moves and abilities**, so your Fakemon's page looks extra fancy.
- **Editing a Fakemon slides in from the right**, and your collection stays right behind it. The finished page lives in the new Preview tab.
- **Every library starts with an "Add new" card**, and custom moves, abilities and items now look like your Fakemon cards and open in a side panel too.
- **Updates and Credits got their own page**, and your account menu (top right) now holds Settings, dark mode and signing in or out.
- **Your Fakemon's page got a glow-up**, on computers and phones. Community posts show the creator right under the Fakemon's name.
- **Big evolution lines stop being so tall.** Eevee-style families fit in a neat grid, with how each one evolves.
- **Browse Abilities** lets you scroll every ability at once, grouped from good to unviable. One click to add!
- **Two new types: ??? and Stellar.**
- **Stat sliders have handles now**, and your arrow keys work on them.
- **Saving your Fakemon as a picture looks the same on every device.** No more squished exports!
- **New icons, a new number font**, and a proper "page not found" screen (it's got the logo and everything).
- The sign-in window got simpler, and a pile of small bugs got squashed along the way.
