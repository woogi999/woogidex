title: Fixed Fakemon Disappearing
---

- Fixed a bug that could silently wipe your whole collection if a rare storage error hit at the wrong moment. Sorry to everyone this happened to.
- If you were affected, we can automatically find and restore a backup copy of what you lost. Look for a pop-up next time you visit.
- You can also trigger this check yourself anytime from Settings > Data > Check for Lost Fakemon.
- Heads up: Woogidex is moving to dex.woogi.xyz soon. A pop-up with move instructions will walk you through bringing your collection over.
