title: Events, Rebuilt From the Ground Up
---

- **Events can be anything now.** Poster competitions, mascot contests, PoA contests, sign-ups for a tournament, you name it. People with the **Organize events** badge can create and run their own events straight from the Community Hub, no admin panel needed.
- **Entry forms work like Google Forms.** Short and long answers, multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdowns, numbers, dates, linear scales, image uploads, links, and page breaks to split a form into steps. Questions can be required or kept private to the event's team, and your email and Discord username fill themselves in if they're on your account.
- **Enter with a whole Fakémon.** Pick one from your collection or upload an exported file, and it goes in with its stats, abilities and moves so it can be judged properly. Event entries don't count toward your Community uploads or cloud storage.
- **Pick how winners are chosen.** A full ballot where everyone rates every entry (the Fakémon-contest scoring is back), a quick community vote, a panel of judges, or no voting at all. Organizers set up the voting form themselves: what it's scored on and out of how much, extra questions for voters, page breaks, whether remarks are needed, and what voters get to see of each entry, including blind judging with names hidden.
- **Winners, your way.** Top N, top percentage, or anyone above a score you choose, plus placements you hand out yourself. Results can be shown live while voting runs, or kept under wraps until a results date you pick.
- **Set it and let it run.** Give an event dates for when entries open and close, when voting opens and closes, and when results come out, and it moves along on its own.
- **Run it with a team.** Add helpers by username and choose what each one can do: edit the event, review entries, judge, or see results. Everything lives in the event's dashboard, with a CSV export of every entry.
- **Open to everyone, if you want.** An event can be opened to people without an account, so the link works like a form anyone can fill in. Those entries go through an anti-spam check and are rate limited, and voting stays members-only.
- **More entries for the people you choose.** Give specific people extra entries, or make an **entry code** and send it to whoever you want.
- **Share it everywhere.** Events get their own custom link, can be reposted or shared to your feed and profile like any post, and organizers can write a results post with full formatting and emojis.
- **Events look the part.** The Events page shows every event as a ticket, and each event has its own tabs for About, Enter, Vote and Results. Organizers get a live preview of their event before publishing, and unfinished events are saved as a draft on your device so nothing gets lost.
- **Formatting got a little fancier.** Posts, comments and event pages now support code blocks, dividing lines (`---`) and ||spoilers||.
- **Battles are taking a break.** The battle simulator was too buggy to be fun, so I've switched it off while I build a better one from the ground up.
- **Woogidex opens even when the servers are down.** Your collection and everything that runs on your device now loads first, instead of waiting on a connection that might never come.
- The buttons on your collection's cards are now stacked vertically.
- The no-artwork placeholder no longer glares on dark mode.
- The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy now speak in first person. Woogidex is a one-person project, so they say "I" instead of "we".
- The "we're moving to a new address" notice is gone, since the move is done.
