version: v1.12.0
date: October 2, 2026
title: Follows, Messages and a Real Feed
---

- **The Community Hub is a feed now.** Scroll through everyone's Fakémon (big pictures, like Instagram) and posts (like Tumblr) all in one place.
- **Post stuff!** Write whatever you want, or show off a bunch of your Fakémon at once ("here's my whole region!!"). Bold, italics, #tags and emojis all work.
- **React to posts** with any of our emojis, and comment on them too.
- **Three ways to read the feed:** For you, Following, and Latest. Curious how For you picks things? It's all explained in plain words, and it's worked out on your own device.
- **Custom emojis, Discord style!** Type `:tatsuorange:` (or just start typing `:tat`) in comments, posts and chats. There's an emoji button too.
- **Follow creators** you like. Their stuff shows up more in your feed and in the Following tab.
- **Profiles got a makeover.** Cover photo, accent colour, pronouns, location, a website link, follower counts, your posts and Fakémon on a timeline, and a wall for people to say hi.
- **Private messages!!** Chat one on one, Messenger style. Send photos, videos, files (up to 10 MB), and voice messages.
- **Group chats.** Make a group with people who follow you.
- **Your messages are end-to-end encrypted.** They're locked on your device before they're sent, so nobody else can read them. Not even us. You'll pick a chat PIN the first time, which unlocks your messages on other devices. Don't lose it!
- **Report a message** if someone's being a jerk. Only that message (and a few before it) gets shared with the mods, nothing else.
- **Block people** if you need to. They can't message you, follow you, or show up in your feed.
- **No spam allowed:** there are limits on how fast you can send messages and files, so the site stays fast for everyone.
- **Inherit moves from prevolutions.** Flip the new switch in the Moves tab and your evolved Fakémon knows everything its earlier forms know. Change the prevolution's moves and it updates by itself.
- **Custom types finally show up right on the Community Hub.** Publishing a Fakémon with a custom type now brings the type's colours and matchups along, so other people don't see it all grey. (Already published something? Hit Update Listing to fix it.)
- **Links look nicer when you share them.** Pasting a Fakémon, post or profile link into Discord and friends shows its name, info and picture instead of the plain homepage.
