title: Sign In With Google, Discord or GitHub
---

- You can now create an account, or sign in, with Google, Discord or GitHub instead of a password. Woogidex only ever asks those services for enough to identify you.
- Already have an account? Settings > Connected Accounts links any of them to it, so you can use either way in. You can disconnect them there too, as long as one way in is left.
- Signing in this way brings your name and picture across as a starting point, and suggests a username. All three are yours to change afterwards.
- The Privacy Policy has been updated to spell out exactly what each provider hands over.
