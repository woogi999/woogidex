version: v1.25.0
date: September 1, 2026
title: Cloud backup and a new community hub
---

- **Cloud backup!** Save a private copy of your collection to your account from **Settings > Data**, so a cleared browser or a new device doesn't take your Fakemon with it. You pick what goes in and what comes back out.
- Backed-up Fakemon get a little cloud icon on their card.
- **The Community Hub has a front page** with a weekly feature, what's trending and what's new. Browsing everything is still one click away.
- **Events and contests** now live inside the Community Hub, and **My uploads** is a new tab for managing what you've published.
- **Artwork is harder to steal.** Community artwork can't be right-clicked and saved any more, but still shows in full quality.
- Likes, comments and views now show on a Fakemon's page too.
- Fixed a few bugs, including the backup meter showing 0 by mistake.
