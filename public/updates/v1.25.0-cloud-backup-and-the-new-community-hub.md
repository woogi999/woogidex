---
version: v1.25.0
date: September 1, 2026
title: Cloud backup and a new community hub
---

- **Cloud backup.** Your collection has always lived only in this browser, which a cleared cache or a new device takes with it. You can now keep a private copy on your account from **Settings > Data**. Only you can see it, it is only written when you press the button, and it holds up to 60 items across your Fakemon, custom moves, abilities and items.
- Pick exactly what goes into the backup and exactly what comes back out of it, with a tab each for Fakemon, moves, abilities and items. Anything already on this device is left ticked off by default; tick it and the backed-up version replaces it, which the picker tells you before you confirm. Nothing is ever deleted.
- Collection cards now carry a small server icon when that Fakemon is backed up. Edit it and the icon goes away, because the backup no longer matches what is on screen.
- The publish button on a collection card is now a back-up button. Publishing to the community moved into the community hub itself.
- **The community hub has a front page.** It opens on a hub with a weekly rotating feature, what is trending, and what was just published, instead of a search bar above every Fakemon ever posted. Browsing is one click away and works exactly as before.
- **Events and contests are part of the hub now** rather than a tab of their own, so a running contest is visible to everyone browsing.
- **My uploads** is a new tab where you can see your listings, update or unpublish them, and publish something new without leaving the hub.
- **Artwork is harder to lift off the page.** Fakemon in the community hub are drawn onto a canvas rather than placed as an image, so there is no picture to right-click and save, nothing to drag to your desktop, and no image address sitting in the element inspector. Your artwork is still uploaded and shown at full resolution, exactly as you drew it.
- **Likes, comments and views now show on a Fakemon's page**, under the author's name, and the heart works there as well as on the card.
- **Importing someone else's listing into your collection, and exporting one to a file, are gone from the community hub.** Both handed over another artist's full-resolution artwork, which rather undid the point of protecting it on the page. Your own Fakemon still import and export exactly as before, from your collection.
- **Your allowance can now differ from everybody else's.** The backup size, the number of community uploads and the wait between publishing are no longer fixed numbers baked into the site. They are settings, and a badge can raise any of them for the people who hold it, including lifting a limit entirely or removing the publishing wait. The meters in Settings show whatever applies to you.
- **Automatic backup**, for accounts whose badge includes it. Switch it on in **Settings > Data** and the entries you already picked are re-uploaded a few minutes after you finish editing them. It never adds anything you did not choose.
- Fixed the cloud backup meter reading 0 on the restore screen. It was following what you had ticked, which on that screen is about your device rather than the backup, so an untouched picker claimed the backup was empty while listing everything in it.
- Fixed the console filling with the same failed request over and over when the connection dropped, and fixed the sidebar being hidden from screen readers on desktop.
