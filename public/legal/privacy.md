# Privacy Policy

*Last updated 31 August 2026*

This Privacy Notice explains how Woogidex ("we", "us", "our") collects, stores, uses and shares your information when you:

- visit our website at **dex.woogi.xyz**, or any site of ours that links to this notice;
- use Woogidex, a browser-based toolkit for making and organising Fakémon; or
- contact us in any other way.

If you do not agree with this notice, please do not use Woogidex. Questions go to **contact@dex.woogi.xyz**.

## Summary of key points

**What we collect.** An account needs a username and a password. An email address is optional, and you can add or remove it at any time. You may also add a display name, an avatar and a bio. Our servers record IP addresses for sign-in attempts and other security events.

**What we do not collect.** We do not process sensitive personal information: no racial or ethnic origin, sexual orientation, religious belief, biometrics or precise location. We do not buy information about you from third parties.

**Do we advertise or track you?** No. Woogidex carries no advertising, no advertising cookies and no third-party analytics. We do not sell or share your personal information, and never will.

**Where your Fakémon live.** Your collection is stored in your own browser. If you are signed in, you can also choose to back it up to your account from **Settings → Data**, so you can get it back on another device. That backup is private to you and is only written when you ask for it.

**Your rights.** You can view, change, export and delete your data yourself from inside the app, and you can delete your whole account from **Settings → Account**. Deletion is scheduled seven days ahead so you can change your mind. See [How long do we keep your information?](#7-how-long-do-we-keep-your-information)

## 1. What information do we collect?

### Information you give us

**Account information.** Creating an account requires a **username** and a **password**. An **email address** is optional. It is only used for password resets and account notices, and you can add or remove it at any time from your profile. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see or store the password itself.

You may instead create an account, or add a second way of signing in to an account you already have, using **Google or Discord**. See [section 6](#6-social-logins) for exactly what we receive when you do.

**Profile information.** You may optionally add a **display name**, an **avatar image** and a **bio**. Everything on your profile is public to other signed-in users.

**Content you create.** Fakémon, custom moves, abilities and items, artwork and cries you upload, battle teams, and anything you publish to the Community Hub, including comments, likes, profile comments and contest entries. Content you publish is visible to other users; content you keep in your collection is not.

Other than the profile details described in [section 6](#6-social-logins), we do not collect sensitive information and we do not collect information about you from third parties.

### Information collected automatically

**Security logs.** When you sign in, sign up, or trigger a rate limit, our servers record the **IP address the request arrived from**, together with a timestamp and the kind of event. The address is read from the request headers, so a browser cannot claim to be somewhere it is not. These records exist to detect brute-force attempts, ban evasion and abuse.

**Battle connections.** Player-versus-player battles connect the two browsers directly using WebRTC. The handshake between them contains **both players' IP addresses**, which is unavoidable for a direct connection. That handshake data is deleted as soon as the battle finishes. When a direct connection is not possible, traffic is relayed through a TURN server (see [section 4](#4-when-and-with-whom-do-we-share-your-information)) instead, which hides your address from your opponent.

**Ordinary server logs.** Like any website, our hosting and database providers keep short-lived request logs containing IP address, browser type and the paths requested.

- **To respond to you** when you contact us.
- **To comply with the law**, or to protect someone's vital interests where there is a risk of harm.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or automated decision-making.

## 4. When and with whom do we share your information?

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising. We rely on the following service providers to run the site, and your information passes through them only to that end:

- **Google and Discord**: only if you choose to sign in with one of them. See [section 6](#6-social-logins).
- **Supabase**: authentication, database and file storage. Your account, profile, collection and published content are stored here.
- **Cloudflare**: hosting and delivery of the website itself.
- **Metered.ca**: TURN relay servers, used only during a player-versus-player battle when a direct connection cannot be made.
- **Google Fonts**: the site's typefaces are loaded from Google's font CDN, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
- **jsDelivr**: a public CDN used for a small number of code libraries.
- **PokéAPI, Pokémon Showdown and PokémonDB**: public reference data and official sprites. Your browser requests these directly; no account information is sent with those requests.

We may also disclose information where we are legally required to, or in connection with a merger, sale of assets or acquisition of our business.

## 5. Cookies and local storage

**Woogidex sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party analytics.** We do not track you across other websites.

We do use your browser's local storage for two things:

- **Your session.** Signing in stores an authentication token so you stay signed in. Clearing it signs you out.
- **Your own data and preferences.** Your Fakémon collection, dark mode, reduced motion, sprite settings and which notices you have dismissed are all kept in your browser.

Clearing your browser storage will sign you out and will delete the local copy of your collection. Export a backup first from **Collection → Export** if that matters to you.

Your collection is not uploaded anywhere unless you ask us to. If you are signed in, **Settings → Data → Cloud Backup** stores a copy of it on our servers so you can get it back on another device; that copy is private to your account, is only written when you press the button, and you can delete it from the same place at any time. It is deleted along with everything else if you delete your account.

## 6. Social logins

You can sign in to Woogidex with **Google or Discord**, and you can connect either of them to an existing account from *Settings → Connected Accounts*.

**What we receive.** When you choose one of these, that provider tells us your **provider account ID**, your **email address**, and, where the provider supplies it, your **name or handle** and your **profile picture**. We store the account ID and email to identify your account on future sign-ins. The name and picture are used only as a starting point: your name becomes your display name, your picture becomes your Woogidex avatar, and the handle is *suggested* to you as a username, which you are free to replace before you accept it. All three are yours to change or clear afterwards from your profile.

**What we do not receive.** We ask each provider for the minimum scope it offers: enough to identify you and nothing else. We do not receive your contacts, your friends or servers, your posts, or the ability to act on your behalf.

**What the provider learns.** Signing in tells that provider that you have an account on Woogidex. Their handling of that is governed by their own privacy policy, not this one.

**Disconnecting.** *Settings → Connected Accounts* removes the connection at any time, provided it is not the only way left into your account. Deleting your Woogidex account ([section 7](#7-how-long-do-we-keep-your-information)) deletes everything we received from the provider along with it; it does not affect your account with the provider itself.

## 7. How long do we keep your information?

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this notice, unless the law requires longer.

- **Your account and its content** are kept for as long as your account exists.
- **Deleting your account** is a two-stage process. When you request deletion from **Settings → Account**, your account is scheduled for permanent deletion **seven days later**. During those seven days you can cancel from the same place and nothing is lost. After seven days your account and everything attached to it (profile, collection, published Fakémon, comments, likes, notifications, battle records and uploaded avatar) is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
- **Security logs** (sign-in attempts and authentication events, with their IP addresses) may be kept beyond account deletion where that is necessary to prevent abuse and ban evasion.
- **Battle handshake data**: which contains IP addresses, is deleted when the battle ends.
- **Moderation records** are retained after deletion in a form that no longer identifies you, so that enforcement history is not lost.

Where information cannot be deleted immediately, for example because it sits in a backup archive, we isolate it from further processing until deletion is possible.

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information: passwords are hashed, all traffic is served over HTTPS, database access is governed by row-level security policies so one account cannot read another's private data, and sign-in attempts are rate limited.

No transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We do our best, but transmission to and from Woogidex is at your own risk, and you should only use the Services in an environment you trust.

## 11. Controls for Do-Not-Track features

Most browsers offer a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") setting. No uniform standard for honouring DNT signals has been agreed, so we do not currently respond to them, though this is somewhat academic here, since Woogidex does not track you across websites in the first place. California law requires us to tell you how we respond to browser DNT signals; the paragraph above is that disclosure.
