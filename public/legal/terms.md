## 6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from **Settings → Account**.

Deletion is scheduled **seven days** in advance. During that week your account keeps working normally and you can cancel the deletion from the same place, with nothing lost. Signing in during the grace period will remind you that a deletion is pending.

After seven days your account and everything attached to it (profile, cloud backup, published Fakémon, comments, likes, notifications, battle records and uploaded avatar) is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. **Export your collection first** from **Collection → Export** if you want to keep a copy.

Some records may be retained after deletion where we are required or permitted to keep them; see [How long do we keep your information?](privacy#7-how-long-do-we-keep-your-information) in the Privacy Policy.

We may also terminate an account for the reasons in [section 5](#5-enforcement).
